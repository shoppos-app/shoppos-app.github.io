Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how the Shop POS app ("the app", "we") handles information. The app helps small shops sell, manage stock and keep records.

1. Data stored on your device

Your shop data — items, prices, sales, customers, debts, suppliers, cash records and settings — is stored on your own device. We do not collect or see this data.

2. Your online account

To manage your subscription, we store your email address, phone number, shop name, owner name, a device identifier, the number of items, and your subscription dates in our account service (Google Firebase). This is used only to run your account, prevent misuse of the free trial, and contact you about your subscription.

3. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app saves backup files of your shop data to a folder called "Shop POS Backups" in your own Google Drive. The app uses the limited drive.file permission, which means it can only see and manage the files it created itself. It cannot see any other files in your Drive. We never receive your backup files. You can stop the automatic backup at any time in the app, and remove the app's access in your Google Account settings.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Camera and Bluetooth

The camera is used only to scan barcodes. Bluetooth is used only to connect to receipt printers. No images or recordings are saved or sent.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone. Messages you send to us (for example on WhatsApp, Telegram or Viber to subscribe) are handled only to process your request.

When customers order from a shop's online page, their name, phone number, address and order are sent only to that shop, so the shop can deliver the order.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete the shop data on your device at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. To delete your online account, email us at s.group.support60@gmail.com or contact us from the app (Settings → Help) and we will remove it.

7. Children

The app is made for shop owners and their staff and is not directed at children.

8. Contact

Email: s.group.support60@gmail.com
Or from inside the app: Settings → Help.
