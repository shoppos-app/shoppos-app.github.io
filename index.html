Frosh POS

Sell faster. Know your stock.

A simple point-of-sale and inventory app for small markets and shops — in English, Arabic and Kurdish (Sorani). Works on Android phones and tablets.

Barcode selling

Scan with the camera or a barcode scanner, see the price and sell in one tap.

Stock & expiry

Buy price, sell price, quantity and expiry date for every item, with low-stock alerts.

Customers & debts

Credit sales, installments and payments, plus suppliers and purchases.

Cash box & delivery

Cash counting, shifts, delivery orders and status tracking.

Reports

Sales, profit and best sellers, exported to PDF, Word or Excel.

Safe backups

Back up to your own Google Drive automatically, and print receipts on Bluetooth printers.
